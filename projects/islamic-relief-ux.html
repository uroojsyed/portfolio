Islamic Relief Canada — Website Redesign

Website accessibility, donor journeys, and data-informed redesign

Figma Crazy Egg Content design User research SEO

Overview

An extensive UX and content overhaul of a national nonprofit website, pairing donor research, usability evaluation and analytics with a practical implementation plan.

Approach & contribution

  1. Audited website usability using heuristics, behaviour analysis, and heatmaps.
  2. Developed donor journeys, user flows, and high-fidelity page concepts.
  3. Structured a phased update plan for 500+ older pages, with accessibility, SEO, content consistency, and QA.

Outcome & status

Following the initial research, usability assessments, and design recommendations, the website redesign underwent further development and refinement before being launched in September 2023. The updated website brought the redesign work into a live digital experience for Islamic Relief Canada's audiences and donors. After launch, the website's performance was monitored and evaluated to ensure that the redesign met the organization's goals for usability, accessibility, and donor engagement. The project was considered a success, with positive feedback from users and stakeholders, as well as improved metrics for user engagement and conversion rates.
