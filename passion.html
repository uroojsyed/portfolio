My creative practice

Art has always been part of my story.

Roohi Studio is my personal art practice, where I explore creativity through traditional media, illustration and original artwork. What began as a passion for creating has evolved into an ongoing space for artistic experimentation and self-expression.

My work reflects an appreciation for composition, detail and visual expression. Through different subjects and techniques, I continue to explore new ideas and develop my artistic style.

While my professional work focuses on digital experiences, information and data, traditional art gives me the freedom to create without those boundaries. It remains an important part of my creative identity.
